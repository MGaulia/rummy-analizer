How to play

Choose separate table and rack photos. Review the local readings and correct their colors and numbers (or jokers), then confirm them. Tap any missed tile to add it. For table tiles, check the suggested meld number and keep their displayed order. Confirm each complete reading before checking or finding a move. An empty table photo is allowed.

Photos are processed only on this device. The local reader is uncalibrated; rotated, overlapping or poorly lit tiles may need manual entry. Reloading clears your readings; offline reopening is not supported by this preview.
